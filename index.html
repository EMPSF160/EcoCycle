The Circular Journey

How Our Recycling Ecosystem Works

From smart electrified pickup to advanced secondary processing and clean energy regeneration.

01

Smart Collection

Our 100% zero-emission electric fleet collects separated bins using route-optimized telematics to minimize transport carbon.

02

Robotic AI Sorting

High-speed optical sensors and robotic arms sort materials by polymer, alloy, and fiber grade with 99.4% purity.

03

Advanced Processing

Materials undergo de-inking, washing, granulation, and pelletizing, preparing pure raw materials for manufacturing.

04

Energy & Reuse

Non-recyclable residues are converted into clean electricity via advanced anaerobic digestion and thermal recovery.
