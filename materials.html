Accepted Materials & Sorting Guide

Learn exactly what can be recycled in our facilities, how to properly prepare items to prevent cross-contamination, and look up individual items with our live interactive tool.

Paper & Cardboard

Fiber Grade

High-tensile kraft fibers recycled into packaging boxes, paper tubes, and newspaper newsprint.

Accepted Materials:
  • • Corrugated shipping boxes
  • • Office copy & printer paper
  • • Newspapers, magazines & brochures
  • • Clean cereal & dry food cartons
  • • Paper egg cartons
Non-Accepted / Prohibited:
  • • Greasy pizza box bottoms
  • • Wax-coated paper cups & plates
  • • Metallic gift wrapping foil
  • • Tissue paper, wipes & napkins
  • • Carbon paper
Preparation Rule:

Flatten all cardboard boxes completely. Remove plastic shipping tape and styrofoam inserts before placing in container.

Plastics & Rigid Polymers

Resin #1, #2, #5

Clean rigid bottles and tubs reprocessed into food-safe circular rPET and rHDPE resin pellets.

Accepted Materials:
  • • PET clear water & soda bottles (#1)
  • • HDPE milk jugs & shampoo bottles (#2)
  • • PP yogurt tubs & food containers (#5)
  • • Clean LDPE pallet wrap films (#4)
  • • Plastic bottle caps (screwed on)
Non-Accepted / Prohibited:
  • • Polystyrene / Styrofoam (#6)
  • • Crisp packets & metallized pouches
  • • Black plastic ready-meal trays
  • • Plastic garden hoses or PVC pipes
  • • Toothpaste tubes
Preparation Rule:

Empty all residual liquids and rinse containers lightly. Screw caps firmly back onto bottles to prevent losing them during mechanical trommel screening.

Glass Bottles & Jars

Infinitely Recyclable

Color-sorted into flint (clear), green, and amber cullet, melted in electric furnaces with zero structural degradation.

Accepted Materials:
  • • Wine, beer & liquor bottles
  • • Jam, pickle & sauce jars
  • • Clear condiment glass bottles
  • • Glass perfume & cosmetic bottles
Non-Accepted / Prohibited:
  • • Pyrex & heat-resistant cookware
  • • Window panes & mirrors
  • • Drinking glasses & crystal
  • • Lightbulbs & fluorescent tubes
Preparation Rule:

Rinse out food residue. Metal lids and corks can be left loose in the glass bin (they are removed by eddy-current and air-density magnets).

Metals & Aluminium

High Energy Savings

Recycling aluminium cans uses 95% less energy than producing virgin aluminium from bauxite ore.

Accepted Materials:
  • • Aluminium drinks cans
  • • Steel & tin food cans
  • • Clean aluminium baking foil
  • • Metal jar lids & crown bottle caps
  • • Empty aerosol cans
Non-Accepted / Prohibited:
  • • Paint tins with wet paint inside
  • • Pressurized gas cylinders & butane
  • • Scrap cutlery & kitchen knives
  • • Barbed wire & heavy rebar
Preparation Rule:

Rinse tins. Roll clean aluminium foil into a ball (golf-ball sized or larger) so sorting blowers detect it reliably.

Electronics & IT (WEEE)

Certified Data Shredding

Precious mineral extraction, circuit board smelting, and certified ISO/ADISA data sanitization.

Accepted Materials:
  • • Laptops, desktops & server racks
  • • Monitors, TVs & display screens
  • • Keyboards, cables & chargers
  • • Small household appliances (toasters, irons)
  • • Telecoms equipment & routers
Non-Accepted in Standard Bin:
  • • Must NOT go in normal wheelie bins!
  • • Damaged or swollen lithium batteries
  • • Industrial radioactive smoke detectors
  • • Uncertified medical X-ray equipment
Preparation Rule:

Book a dedicated WEEE electronic collection box or pallet drop. Hard drives can be tracked with serialized audit destruction logs.

Batteries & Energy Cells

Hazardous Control

Specialist hydrometallurgical processing to recover lithium, cobalt, nickel, and manganese safely without thermal runaway.

Accepted Materials:
  • • Standard alkaline (AA, AAA, 9V, C, D)
  • • Button cell watch & hearing aid cells
  • • Lithium-ion laptop & phone packs
  • • Power tool battery packs
  • • Automotive 12V lead-acid batteries
Non-Accepted / Strict Warning:
  • • Never place batteries in mixed recycling!
  • • Punctured or smoking batteries
  • • Corroded acid leakage without tub
  • • High-voltage grid utility packs
Preparation Rule:

Place insulating tape over positive terminals of 9V and lithium batteries. Use our flame-retardant battery deposit caddies.

Organic & Food Waste

Anaerobic Bio-Energy

Digested in sealed bio-reactors, generating renewable electricity and high-nutrient agricultural bio-fertilizer.

Accepted Materials:
  • • Fruit, vegetable peels & cores
  • • Coffee grounds & paper filter bags
  • • Cooked & raw food leftovers
  • • Meat scraps & small bones
  • • Certified compostable liners (EN 13432)
Non-Accepted / Prohibited:
  • • Standard plastic shopping bags
  • • Glass, metal, or cutlery items
  • • Dog or cat pet waste
  • • Cooking oil in bulk liquid drums
Preparation Rule:

Line kitchen caddies exclusively with 100% compostable certified bags. Never tie using standard polyethylene twist ties.

Have Questionable or Unlisted Materials?

Our laboratory waste chemists can test and classify unknown industrial compounds or provide customized containment bins.
