The Four-Step Recycling Journey & Impact

Discover the engineering precision that powers our zero-landfill guarantee. From smart collection telemetry to automated polymer optical separation and clean energy export.

01
Stage 1: Clean Logistics

Waste Collection & Route Telemetry

Every collection begins with route-optimized scheduling powered by our electrified fleet.

Our heavy refuse vehicles and urban sprinter vans are 100% electric and biogas powered. Ultrasonic fill-level sensors installed inside commercial containers transmit live volume metrics to our dispatch center, triggering pickups only when bins reach 80% capacity, thereby cutting haulage carbon emissions by 34%.

Electrified Fleets IoT Ultrasonic Bin Sensors Dynamic Route Optimization
02
Stage 2: High Purity

Material Sorting & Optical Robotics

Robotic AI arms and near-infrared optical sensors categorize up to 12 tonnes per hour.

Upon entering our Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs), waste passes through rotary ballistic separators and vibrating screens. High-speed cameras identify materials by spectral signature, firing compressed-air nozzles to eject PET, HDPE, aluminium, or cardboard onto dedicated streams with 99.4% purity.

NIR Hyperspectral Cameras High-Speed Air Ejectors Robotic Delta Pickers
03
Stage 3: Refinement

Cleaning, Pelletizing & Processing

Decontaminating materials and returning them to virgin-grade manufacturing specifications.

Plastics are shredded into uniform flakes, hot-washed in caustic baths to remove adhesive labels, and extruded into uniform polymer pellets ready for food-safe bottle manufacturing. Paper and cardboard are hydro-pulped and screened to remove ink, while metals are crushed into dense mill-ready briquettes.

Closed-Loop Water Scrubbing Food-Grade rPET Pellets Mill-Spec Balers
04
Stage 4: Regeneration

Recycling, Remanufacture & Clean Energy

Closing the loop with circular supply chains and powering regional grids with renewable energy.

Recovered materials return directly to UK and European packaging manufacturers, eliminating virgin resource extraction. Any residual non-recyclable organics enter our anaerobic digestion tanks, capturing bio-methane that powers gas turbines and exports over 140GWh of clean green electricity annually to the national grid.
