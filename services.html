Comprehensive Circular Waste Services

From neighborhood municipal collections to heavy industrial zero-landfill infrastructure, we deliver certified closed-loop resource logistics for every sector.

Municipal & Residential

1. Residential Recycling

Quiet, zero-emission municipal wheelie bin collections for urban councils and residential communities. Includes dual-stream food waste, mixed paper, glass, and domestic plastics with smart RFID tracking on all bins.

  • 100% Electric, low-noise collection fleet
  • Missed bin alerts & route notification SMS
  • Zero-contamination resident educational packs

Commercial & Enterprise

2. Commercial Recycling

Scalable waste management designed for office buildings, retail high streets, hospitality venues, and corporate headquarters. Includes internal multi-stream separation stations and verified monthly ESG diversion certificates.

  • 240L to 1100L commercial wheeled bins
  • Timed out-of-hours collection windows
  • Audited carbon savings certificates for ESG reporting

Heavy Industry

3. Industrial Waste Management

Engineered solutions for manufacturing plants, automotive factories, chemical producers, and construction contractors. We supply heavy compactors, balers, and roll-on roll-off containers with full hazardous waste duty-of-care consignment notes.

  • Heavy roll-on roll-off (RoRo) containers (20–40 cu yd)
  • Hazardous materials consignment compliance
  • On-site compaction machinery leasing & maintenance
Custom Contract / 24/7 Logistics Enquire For Industrial
Polymer Science

4. Plastic Polymer Recycling

Advanced optical sorting and high-purity reprocessing of post-consumer and post-industrial polymers. We process PET bottles, HDPE jugs, PP crates, and LDPE clear shrink film, converting them into circular food-grade pellets and flannels.

  • NIR (Near-Infrared) laser spectrum separation
  • Hot-wash decontamination & friction scrubbing
  • Traceable European chemical standard compliance
High-Purity Resin Feedstocks Enquire For Plastics
High Tech & WEEE

5. Electronic Waste (WEEE) Collection

Secure lifecycle retirement for enterprise IT infrastructure, telecom equipment, server racks, laptops, and consumer electronics. Features ADISA and NIST 800-88 certified hard drive degaussing and precision precious metals extraction.

  • Guaranteed digital data sanitization & shredding
  • Serialized certificates of disposal for compliance
  • Gold, copper, and rare earth element recovery
Secure Chain-Of-Custody Enquire For WEEE
Fiber Recovery

6. Paper & Cardboard Baling

Closed-loop fiber solutions for logistics hubs, distribution warehouses, retail chains, and print houses. We supply high-density on-site balers, collect consolidated mill-size bales, and deliver pulp back to paper packaging mills.

  • Mill-size 500kg bale collections with rebate options
  • Clean confidential document shredding (BS EN 15713)
  • 100% repulping in UK and European paper mills
Volume Rebates Available Enquire For Cardboard
Infinite Materials

7. Metal & Glass Recycling

Ferrous and non-ferrous metals (aluminium, copper, steel, brass) and color-separated glass cullet. Both metals and glass can be melted and remanufactured indefinitely with zero loss in structural integrity, saving up to 95% of virgin smelting energy.

  • Eddy-current magnetic aluminium and steel separators
  • Optical color-sorted cullet (clear, green, amber)
  • Scrap metal weighbridge certification & payments
100% Infinitely Recyclable Enquire For Metals & Glass
Logistics & Skips

8. Bulk Waste Collection & Skips

Rapid response skip hire, grab lorry services, and scheduled bulky clearing for commercial fit-outs, facility refurbishments, and industrial site clearances. Every skip load is hand-sorted and mechanically screened at our transfer depots.

  • 4-yard to 40-yard skips and hook loaders
  • Guaranteed same-day or next-day delivery options
  • Full site waste management plans (SWMP) provided
